> [!summary]  
> Diffuse, intense skeletal tracer uptake on bone scintigraphy with a high bone-to-background ratio and faint or absent urinary tract activity, usually due to metabolic bone disease or diffuse osteoblastic metastases.

---
## Differential diagnosis

Distribution favours an aetiology but is not diagnostic.

| Diffuse osteoblastic metastases | [[00_Inbox/Metabolic bone disease\|Metabolic bone disease]]                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can be patchy or asymmetric     | Usually symmetric                                                            |
| Predominant axial involvement   | Often involves skull and mandible, and potentially distal appendicular bones |

> [!warning]
> - "Normal" bone scan may be a superscan → assess skeletal uptake, soft-tissue background and renal activity together.
> - Reduced renal activity alone is non-specific; renal insufficiency can also cause poor renal visualisation.
