Spectrum includes Atypical Lobular Hyperplasia (ALH) and Lobular Carcinoma In Situ (LCIS).
* **Pathology:** Loss of **E-cadherin** expression (CDH1 mutation).
* **Clinical Significance:**
    * Classic LCIS: Marker of generalized increased risk (bilateral).
    * Pleomorphic LCIS: More aggressive, treated like high-grade Ductal Carcinoma In Situ (DCIS).
* **Imaging:** Often incidental; LCIS rarely calcifies (unlike DCIS).
