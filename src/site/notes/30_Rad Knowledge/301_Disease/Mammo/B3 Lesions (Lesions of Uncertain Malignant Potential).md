## General concept
* **Definition**: Breast lesions identified on Core Needle Biopsy (CNB) that are **benign** but have a heterogeneous histology with an increased risk of associated malignancy (DCIS or invasive cancer) in the surrounding tissue ("upgrade rate") or an increased long-term risk of developing breast cancer.
* **Histological Classification (NHS BSP)**:
    * **B3a**: Without epithelial atypia (e.g., typical [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Intraductal papilloma\|papilloma]], [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Radial scar\|Radial scar]], cellular [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroepithelial lesions\|Fibroepithelial lesions]]).
    * **B3b**: With epithelial atypia (e.g., [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Atypical ductal hyperplasia\|ADH]], classic LCIS, FEA).

## Specific entities

### 1. Atypical Intraductal Proliferation (AIDP)
* **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Atypical ductal hyperplasia\|Atypical ductal hyperplasia]] (ADH)**:

### 2. Lobular Neoplasia (LN)
* Spectrum: **Atypical Lobular Hyperplasia (ALH)** and **Lobular Carcinoma in Situ (LCIS)**.
* **Classic LCIS**:
    * Generalized risk factor (marker) rather than a direct precursor (though some debate exists).
    * **Management**: Diagnostic excision (VAE or open) to rule out synchronous cancer. If concordant and classic type: **Surveillance** (Annual mammography).
* **Pleomorphic LCIS / Florid LCIS**:
    * Behaves like high-grade [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Ductal carcinoma in situ\|DCIS]] (comedo-necrosis).
    * **Management**: **Surgical excision** with clear margins is mandatory (treated like DCIS).

### 3. Flat Epithelial Atypia (FEA)
* **Definition**: Dilated ducts lined by a single layer of columnar cells with low-grade atypia (Columnar Cell Change with Atypia).
* **Association**: Frequently associated with low-grade DCIS and [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Tubular carcinoma\|Tubular carcinoma]].
* **Upgrade rate**: Lower (~5–10%).
* **Management**: VAE. If no malignancy found on VAE, surveillance is appropriate.

### 4. Papillary lesions
* **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Intraductal papilloma\|Intraductal papilloma]]**.

### 5. Radial Scar / CSL
* See [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Radial scar\|Radial scar]].
* B3a (if no atypia) or B3b (if associated with ADH/FEA).
* **Management**: Excision required (VAE or surgery).

### 6. [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroepithelial lesions\|Fibroepithelial lesions]] (Cellular)
* Distinction between **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|Fibroadenoma]]** and **Benign [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Phyllodes tumor\|Phyllodes tumor]]** can be difficult on CNB.
* If reported as "Cellular fibroepithelial lesion" or "Phyllodes cannot be excluded":
    * **Management**: Excision (usually surgical with margins for Phyllodes).

## Management algorithm (NHS BSP / RCR Consensus)

> [!important] The "Second-Line" Procedure
> A B3 result on CNB **always** triggers further action. Discharge is **never** the immediate next step.

1.  **MDT Discussion**: Mandatory. Radiologic-pathologic correlation is critical.
2.  **Vacuum Assisted Excision (VAE)**:
    * *First-line* for most B3 lesions ([[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Intraductal papilloma\|papilloma]], [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Radial scar\|Radial scar]], FEA, classic LCIS) to obtain larger tissue volume (4g tissue or clear radiologic removal).
    * **Therapeutic**: Removes the lesion entirely.
    * **Diagnostic**: Excludes adjacent malignancy.
3.  **Surgical Excision**:
    * Indicated for **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Atypical ductal hyperplasia\|ADH]]** (standard care), **Pleomorphic LCIS**, **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Phyllodes tumor\|Phyllodes tumor]]**, or if VAE is technically not feasible (lesion too posterior/thin breast).
    * Indicated if VAE pathology shows upgrade to malignancy.
4.  **Surveillance**:
    * Only initiated **after** VAE/Surgery confirms no invasive cancer or [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Ductal carcinoma in situ\|DCIS]].
    * Protocol: Annual mammography for 5 years (standard UK protocol).

## Exam pearls
* **Common Scenario**: 50F, screening mammogram shows microcalcifications. CNB shows FEA.
    * *Next step*: **VAE**. (Reason: FEA is associated with low-grade [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Ductal carcinoma in situ\|DCIS]]/[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Tubular carcinoma\|Tubular carcinoma]] in adjacent tissue).
* **The "Upgrade" Trap**:
    * Exam questions often ask for the "highest risk of upgrade".
    * **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Atypical ductal hyperplasia\|ADH]]** usually has the highest upgrade rate among B3 lesions (up to 30%).
* **Mucocele-like lesions (MLL)**:
    * Rare B3 lesion.
    * Mucin-filled cysts requiring differentiation from [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Disease/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Mucinous carcinoma\|Mucinous carcinoma]].
    * If atypia present -> Excision.
* **Discordance**: If imaging looks malignant (BI-RADS 5) but biopsy is B3 -> **Diagnostic Surgical Excision** is mandatory regardless of B3 subtype.
