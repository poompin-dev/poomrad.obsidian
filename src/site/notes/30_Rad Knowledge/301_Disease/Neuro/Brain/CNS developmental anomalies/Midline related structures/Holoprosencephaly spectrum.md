A spectrum of malformations caused by failure of midline cleavage of prosencephalon (forebrain)
## Clinical features
- Severe mental retard
## Association
- Facial dysmorphism, very common (~80%)
	- Hypotelorism
	- Cyclopia
	- Cebocephaly
	- Single maxillary central incisor
	- Midline cleft lip/palate
---
## Imaging hallmark
- Abnormal communication of gray and white matter across midline
## Classification
- Alobar: most severe
	- Complete absent of cleavage
	+ Central monoventricle expanding posteriorly into a dorsal cyst.
- Semilobar
	- Separation of the temporal lobes
	- Posterior lateral ventricles can be identified.
- Lobar
	- Anterior falx cerebri can be partially present.
	- Presence of splenium of the corpus callosum
- Middle interhemispheric variant (syntelencephaly)
	- Lack of separation of the posterior frontal and parietal regions
	- Cleavage of the anterior frontal and occipital lobes

> [!note]
> - Absent of anterior falx cerebri and azygos ACA can be seen in alobar and semilobar types.
> - Lobar vs semilobar can be somewhat arbitrary.
