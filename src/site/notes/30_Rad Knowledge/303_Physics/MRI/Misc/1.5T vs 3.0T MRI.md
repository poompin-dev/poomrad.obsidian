> [!summary] Main ideas
> - 3.0T is not automatically better than 1.5T
> - 3.0T provides higher SNR per scan time in exchange for more cost/technical demand, more proning to specific artefacts, and more safety issues.


## Clinical comparison

| Feature               | 1.5T                                                                                                        | 3.0T                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cost and availability | Lower cost; widely available                                                                                | Higher cost; more common in tertiary/high-volume centres                                                                                                                        |
| Implants and devices  | More forgiving; many legacy MR-conditional pathways are 1.5T-based                                          | Must check exact MR-conditional labelling; 1.5T clearance does not imply 3T clearance                                                                                           |
| Cardiac               | Workhorse for [[30_Rad Knowledge/303_Physics/MRI/Sequences/bSSFP\|bSSFP]] cine; less [[30_Rad Knowledge/303_Physics/MRI/Artefacts/Banding artefact\|banding artefact]], easier ECG gating, lower SAR burden                | Higher SNR; useful for perfusion, LGE; bSSFP more banding, needs shimming/frequency scouting                                                                                    |
| Neuro                 | Adequate for most routine brain/spine MRI                                                                   | Preferred for fMRI, DTI, perfusion, SWI, MRS, epilepsy protocols                                                                                                                |
| Small structures      | Adequate but lower SNR reserve                                                                              | Preferred; benefits from SNR reserve traded for resolution                                                                                                                      |
| Body                  | More forgiving with large FOV, ascites, and large body habitus, fewer dielectric/B1 issues                  | More common in tertiary centres; higher SNR but more B1 inhomogeneity, dielectric shading, susceptibility, chemical shift; multi-transmit helps but does not eliminate problems |
| Pregnancy             | Common practical preference if clinically justified                                                         | Acceptable upto 3T in Normal Operating Mode when indicated                                                                                                                      |
| Prostate              | Acceptable in modern 1.5T system with good phased-array coils; older systems may need endorectal coil (ERC) | PI-RADS v2.1 preferred 3T due to superior SNR without ERC needed                                                                                                                |
| MRA                   | Adequate contrast-enhanced MRA                                                                              | Higher SNR helps non-contrast MRA and accelerated/high-resolution MRA                                                                                                           |
| Scan time/resolution  | Longer for equivalent SNR/resolution                                                                        | SNR reserve can be traded for speed, resolution, thinner slices, or acceleration                                                                                                |

> [!tip] Pearls
>- **Cardiac cine = 1.5T advantage** because bSSFP banding/off-resonance is worse at 3T.
>- **Prostate, epilepsy, cranial nerves, cartilage, SWI/MRS/DTI/fMRI = 3T advantage** because of SNR/resolution.
>- **Pregnancy/large ascites/large body habitus = 1.5T is more forgiving**, but 3T is not automatically contraindicated.
>- **Implants: never say “safe at 1.5T therefore safe at 3T.”** Field-strength-specific labelling is the key.
