## Clinical comparison

| Feature                           | 1.5T                                                                        | 3.0T                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cost and availability             | Lower capital and running cost; widely available                            | Higher cost; concentrated in tertiary centres                                               |
| Implants and devices              | More MR-conditional devices; less RF heating in elongated metallic implants | Many implants restricted or contraindicated; check vendor labelling carefully               |
| Cardiac                           | Workhorse for [[30_Rad Knowledge/303_Physics/MRI/Sequences/bSSFP\|bSSFP]] cine (less [[30_Rad Knowledge/303_Physics/MRI/Artefacts/Banding artefact\|banding artefact]], lower SAR)         | Better for LGE, T1/T2 mapping, parametric imaging; bSSFP needs wideband or careful shimming |
| Neuro                             | Adequate for most routine protocols                                         | Preferred for fMRI, DTI, perfusion, SWI, MRS, epilepsy protocols                            |
| Small structures                  | Limited (inner ear, cranial nerves, cartilage)                              | Preferred; benefits from SNR reserve traded for resolution                                  |
| Body (liver, MRCP, pancreas, MRE) | More forgiving with large FOV, ascites, pregnancy                           | Now standard in most tertiary centres; multi-transmit RF largely solves dielectric artefact |
| Pregnancy and large body habitus  | Preferred (less dielectric artefact, lower SAR)                             | Possible with multi-transmit but more artefact-prone                                        |
| Prostate                          | Acceptable with endorectal coil                                             | Preferred (PI-RADS v2.1 favours 3T); better small-structure resolution                      |
| Paediatrics and claustrophobia    | Loud, but has shorter bore options                                          | Louder                                                                                      |
| MRA                               | Adequate contrast-enhanced MRA                                              | Better non-contrast techniques                                                              |
| Scan time                         | Longer for equivalent SNR                                                   | Shorter, can trade SNR for resolution or acceleration                                       |
