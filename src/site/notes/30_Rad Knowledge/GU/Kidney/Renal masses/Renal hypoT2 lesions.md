## Differential
### 1. [[30_Rad Knowledge/GU/Kidney/Renal masses/Renal cell carcinoma#Papillary\|Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC), Papillary Subtype]]
   - **Description**: Papillary RCC typically appears hypointense on T2-weighted imaging compared to other RCC subtypes.
### 2. Hemorrhagic or Proteinaceous Cyst
   - **Description**: Simple renal cysts usually appear hyperintense on T2, but if a cyst contains blood (hemorrhagic) or proteinaceous material, it can appear hypointense.
   - **Clinical Significance**: These cysts are usually benign but can be mistaken for solid tumors if not properly characterized with contrast-enhanced imaging.
### 3. Lipid-Poor [[30_Rad Knowledge/GU/Kidney/Renal masses/Renal angiomyolipoma\|Renal angiomyolipoma]] (AML)
   - **Description**: Classic AMLs are hyperintense on T2 due to their fat content, but lipid-poor AMLs lack the characteristic fat and can appear hypointense on T2.
   - **Clinical Significance**: AMLs are benign, but larger ones can cause bleeding. Lipid-poor AMLs may be more difficult to distinguish from malignant lesions.
