## Definition
Monomorphic proliferation of epithelial cells filling <2 duct spaces or <2 mm in size (morphologically similar to low-grade [[30_Rad Knowledge/Mammo/Epithelial breast neoplasms/Ductal carcinoma in situ\|DCIS]] but not full-fill cutoff criteria)
## Significance
Highest upgrade rate to malignancy (approx. **15–30%**) among [[30_Rad Knowledge/Mammo/B3 Lesions (Lesions of Uncertain Malignant Potential)\|B3]] lesions (RR ~4-5x).
## Management
* **Surgical excision** is the standard NCCN recommendation.
* Recent UK guidelines allow Vacuum Assisted Excision (VAE) if < 4mm and completely excised, but Surgery remains the "safe" exam answer for high-risk atypia.
