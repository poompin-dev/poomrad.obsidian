Formerly Invasive Ductal Carcinoma (IDC). Accounts for 70-80% of all invasive breast cancers.
## Radiological features:
* **Mammography:** Irregular, high-density mass with spiculated margins. Associated pleomorphic calcifications common.
* **US:** Hypoechoic, irregular mass, **anti-parallel orientation** (taller-than-wide), posterior acoustic shadowing, echogenic halo (desmoplastic reaction).
* **MRI:** Rapid initial enhancement with washout (Type 3 curve) or plateau (Type 2 curve). Rim enhancement is a specific sign of malignancy.
