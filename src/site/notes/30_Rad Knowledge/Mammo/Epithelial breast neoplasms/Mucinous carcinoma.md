* **Population:** Elderly women.
* **Imaging:**
    * **Mammography:** Circumscribed, low-density mass (can mimic benign cyst/[[30_Rad Knowledge/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|fibroadenoma]]).
    * **MRI:** **Very high T2 signal** (due to mucin). Enhances (differentiation from cyst).
