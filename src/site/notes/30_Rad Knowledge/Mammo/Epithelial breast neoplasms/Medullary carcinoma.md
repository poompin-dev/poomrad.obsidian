* **Association:** *BRCA1* carriers, younger patients.
* **Histology:** High-grade, lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate.
* **Imaging:** Circumscribed mass (mimics [[30_Rad Knowledge/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|fibroadenoma]]). US may show posterior acoustic enhancement (necrosis/cellularity) rather than shadowing.
