* **Prognosis:** Excellent (Grade 1).
* **Imaging:** Small, spiculated mass. Differential: [[30_Rad Knowledge/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Radial scar\|Radial scar]].
* **Exam Tip:** "Radially spiculated mass with central lucency" usually favors radial scar, but tubular carcinoma can mimic perfectly. Biopsy essential.
