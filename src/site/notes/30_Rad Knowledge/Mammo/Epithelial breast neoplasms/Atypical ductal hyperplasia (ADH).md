* **Definition:** Monomorphic proliferation of epithelial cells filling <2 duct spaces or <2 mm in size.
* **Risk:** Increases relative risk of breast cancer (RR ~4-5x).
* **Management:** Surgical excision recommended (upgrade rate to malignancy ~10-20% on vacuum-assisted biopsy).
