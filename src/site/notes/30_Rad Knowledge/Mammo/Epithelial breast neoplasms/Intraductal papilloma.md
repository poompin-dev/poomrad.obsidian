* **Pathology:** Proliferation of ductal epithelium on fibrovascular core. Common cause of pathologic nipple discharge.
* **Imaging:**
    * *Mammography:* Often occult; may show solitary dilated retroareolar duct or circumscribed mass.
    * *Ultrasound (US):* Solid nodule within a fluid-filled duct (intracystic) or solid ductal mass.
    * *Galactography:* Filling defect (meniscus sign).
* **Management:** Biopsy required to exclude papillary carcinoma (lack of myoepithelial layer).
