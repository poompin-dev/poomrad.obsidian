* **Definition:** Neoplastic epithelial proliferation confined to the ductal-lobular system; no basement membrane invasion.
* **Grading:** Low, Intermediate, High (nuclear grade).
* **Imaging:**
    * *Mammography:* **Microcalcifications** (70-80% of cases). Morphology: Pleomorphic, fine linear branching (casting), or crushed stone.
    * *MRI:* Non-mass enhancement (segmental or ductal distribution).
* **Exam Tip:** High-grade DCIS often associated with "comedo necrosis" (central necrosis within ducts).
