Accounts for 5-15%. Cells lack E-cadherin (non-cohesive).
* **Growth Pattern:** "Indian file" (single file) infiltration; preserves architecture (little desmoplasia).
* **Imaging Challenges:**
    * **Mammography:** High false-negative rate. May present as subtle **architectural distortion** rather than a mass. "Shrinking breast" sign rare but classic.
    * **US:** Ill-defined area of shadowing without distinct mass.
    * **MRI:** Most sensitive modality. often non-mass enhancement.
* **Metastasis Pattern:** Peritoneum, retroperitoneum, GI tract, gynecologic organs (different from NST which favors lung/liver/bone).
