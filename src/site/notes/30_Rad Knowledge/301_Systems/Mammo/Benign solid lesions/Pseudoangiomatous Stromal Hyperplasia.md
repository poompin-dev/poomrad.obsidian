Hormonally stimulated myofibroblastic proliferation.
* **Pathology:** Anastomosing slit-like spaces lined by myofibroblasts (mimics angiosarcoma histologically but is benign).
* **Imaging:** Non-specific. Usually looks like a [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|Fibroadenoma]] or developing asymmetry.
* **Management:** Biopsy required. Excision if growing; otherwise observation.
