* **Pathology:** Pure epithelial proliferation (closely packed tubules) with minimal stroma.
* **Imaging:** Indistinguishable from non-calcified [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|Fibroadenoma]].
* **Clinical:** Younger women. Palpable, mobile mass.
* **Key differentiator:** Does **not** develop large "popcorn" calcifications (unlike FA).
