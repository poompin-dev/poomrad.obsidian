Benign proliferation of fibrous, glandular, and fatty tissue (disorganized but mature).
* **Mammography:**
    * **"Breast within a breast" sign:** Radiolucent fat mixed with radiodense glandular tissue surrounded by a pseudocapsule.
    * **"Salami slice" appearance.**
* **US:** Heterogeneous compressible mass.
* **Management:** BIRADS 2 (Benign). No biopsy needed if classic appearance.
