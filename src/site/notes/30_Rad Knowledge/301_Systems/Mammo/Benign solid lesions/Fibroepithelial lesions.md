Fibroepithelial lesions are biphasic tumors characterized by proliferation of both **stromal** and **epithelial** components.
- [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Fibroadenoma\|Fibroadenoma]]
- [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Phyllodes tumor\|Phyllodes tumor]]
- [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Mammo/Benign solid lesions/Tubular adenoma\|Tubular adenoma]]

**Key Exam Challenge:** Differentiating cellular fibroadenoma from benign phyllodes tumor (often impossible on imaging; requires excision).
