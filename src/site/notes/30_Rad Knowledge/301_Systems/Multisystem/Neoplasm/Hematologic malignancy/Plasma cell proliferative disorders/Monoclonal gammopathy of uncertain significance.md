Part of [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Multisystem/Neoplasm/Hematologic malignancy/Plasma cell proliferative disorders/Plasma cell proliferative disorders\|plasma cell proliferative disorders]]

Precursor to multiple myeloma defined by detectable abnormal M-protein (serum or urine) but otherwise no other criteria
- Bone marrow: **<10%** clonal plasma cells
- **No CRAB** features (no hypercalcemia, renal dysfunction, anemia, or bone lesions)
## Prognosis
Risk of progression to MM: **~1% per year**
