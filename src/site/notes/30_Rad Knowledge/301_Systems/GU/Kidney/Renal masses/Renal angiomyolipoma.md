## Overview
- The most common **benign** tumor of the kidney.
- Essentially a renal [[Harmartoma\|harmatoma]].
- Almost all (95%) have macroscopic fat.
- Usually incidental findings.

## Clinical significance
- Assoc. with [[Tuberous Sclerosis Complex (TSC)\|Tuberous sclerosis complex]]
- Bleed if size > 4 cm
- Never have calcification (if have considered [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/GU/Kidney/Renal masses/Renal cell carcinoma\|Renal cell carcinoma]])
## Zebra
5% of renal AML are lipid poor. More commonly seen in [[Tuberous Sclerosis Complex (TSC)\|TSC]].
- Have to ddx with papillary [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/GU/Kidney/Renal masses/Renal cell carcinoma\|RCC]]. 
- See also, [[Ddx of renal hypoT2 lesions\|Ddx of renal hypoT2 lesions]]
