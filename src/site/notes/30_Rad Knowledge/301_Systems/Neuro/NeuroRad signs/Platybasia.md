## Diagnosis
- Abnormal flattening of the skull base, defined as a base of skull angle > 143º.
	- Angle formed by a line joining the **nasion** with the **centre of the pituitary fossa** and a line joining the centre of the pituitary fossa with the **basion**.
- Often coexists with [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Neuro/Head and neck/Skull base/Basilar invagination\|Basilar invagination]].
