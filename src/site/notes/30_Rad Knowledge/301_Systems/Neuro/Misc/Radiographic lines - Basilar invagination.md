Five lines have been used to evaluate [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Neuro/Head and neck/Skull base/Basilar invagination\|Basilar invagination]] on frontal and lateral images, based on the relative projection of the odontoid process to these lines.
## Lateral image



|                        | Reference points                                                    | Basilar invagination threshold    | Misc.                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **McRae line**         | basion to the opisthion                                             | tip of the dens crosses this line | [[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Neuro/Brain/CNS developmental anomalies/Posterior fossa/Chiari I malformation\|Chiari I]]: cerebellar tonsils position below >5 mm with associated findings/symptoms. |
| **Chamberlain's line** | back of hard palate to the opisthion                                | tip of the dens above >3 mm       | #ThAC                                                                                                           |
| **McGregor line**      | back of hard palate to the most caudal point of the occipital curve | tip of the dens above >4.5-5 mm   | modification of Chamberlain line, used when opisthion cannot be identify                                        |

## Frontal image



|                    | Reference points                       | Basilar invagination threshold    | Misc.                         |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Digastric line** | right and left digastric grooves       | tip of the dens **below** < 11 mm | also called **biventer line** |
| **Bimastoid line** | inferior tips of the mastoid processes | tip of the dens **above** >10 mm  |                               |
