Wormian bones = **extra ossicles within cranial sutures**, especially lambdoid.

They can be normal variants, **but multiple (>10) or very large ones → think underlying disease.**

1. Collagen / bone dysplasia group
	- Osteogenesis imperfecta (#1 association)
	- Cleidocranial dysplasia
	- Pyknodysostosis
	- Hypophosphatasia
2. Metabolic
	- Rickets
	- Hypothyroidism (cretinism)
3. Syndromic
	- Down syndrome
	- Menkes disease (kinky hair)
