> [!info] Key points
> 
> - **4D = 3D + time** (contrast kinetics across multiple phases)
> - Classic adenoma: **low on NCCT**, **avid arterial enhancement**, **rapid washout** on venous/delayed
> - Adenoma is **hypodense to thyroid on NCCT** (thyroid is hyperdense due to iodine content)
> - **Polar vessel sign**: enlarged feeding artery off superior/inferior thyroid artery
> - Use when US/sestamibi negative, discordant, or for re-exploration; sensitivity ~85-90% for single adenoma

---
## Indications
- Pre-op localisation in **primary [[31_Medical knowledge/Endocrinology/Parathyroid hormone/Hyperparathyroidism signpost\|hyperparathyroidism]] (PHPT)** planned for minimally invasive parathyroidectomy
- Non-localising or discordant US/sestamibi
- **Re-operative cases** (best modality in this setting)
- Suspected ectopic adenoma
- Suspected multiglandular disease

PHPT aetiology: ~85% solitary adenoma, ~10-15% multigland hyperplasia, <1% carcinoma.

---
## Protocol
- **Coverage**: angle of mandible to carina (must include mediastinum for ectopic inferior glands)
- **Phases**:
    - **Non-contrast** (crucial for thyroid discrimination)
    - **Arterial** (~25-30 s, or bolus-tracked)
    - **Venous** (~60-80 s)
    - ± **Delayed** (~90 s, often dropped to reduce dose)
- Thin slices (1-1.5 mm), axial + coronal + sagittal MPR
- Some centres use bi-phasic protocols (NC + arterial only) to limit thyroid dose, especially in young patients

---
## Relevant anatomy
- Typically **4 glands**; ~10% have supernumerary glands
- **Superior glands** (4th pharyngeal pouch): more constant; posterior to mid/upper thyroid in tracheo-oesophageal groove
- **Inferior glands** (3rd pharyngeal pouch, descends with thymus): more variable; near lower thyroid pole or anywhere along thymic tract
- **Ectopic sites**: intrathyroidal, retro-oesophageal, anterior mediastinum (thymic), carotid sheath, undescended high (near hyoid), rarely intravagal

---
## Imaging features of adenoma

### Non-contrast
- **Hypodense to thyroid** (thyroid bright due to iodine)
- Typically ovoid, 1-2 cm, soft tissue density

### Arterial
- **Avid enhancement**, usually greater than thyroid on arterial phase
- Peaks early

### Venous / delayed
- **Washout** (more rapid than thyroid)
- Re-becomes hypodense relative to thyroid

### Ancillary signs
- **Polar vessel sign**: prominent feeding artery from inferior or superior thyroid artery
- **Cleavage/fat plane** between adenoma and thyroid (helps distinguish from exophytic thyroid nodule)
- Cystic change occasionally; more typical of hyperplasia or carcinoma

### Quantitative tips
- Most discriminating metric: **large attenuation increase from NC to arterial** (adenomas enhance briskly; nodes do not)
- Arterial-phase HU of adenoma typically > thyroid; in nodes, arterial HU < thyroid
- Washout ratios (arterial - venous / arterial) also used in some protocols

---
## Differentials

|Mimic|Distinguishing feature|
|---|---|
|Cervical lymph node|No washout, fatty hilum, chain distribution, less avid arterial|
|Exophytic thyroid nodule|Contiguous with thyroid, no cleavage plane, similar kinetics to thyroid|
|Thymic remnant|Mediastinal, longitudinal, follows thymic density|
|Schwannoma|Carotid sheath, heterogeneous, no avid arterial peak|
|Zenker diverticulum|Contains air/fluid/debris, connects to pharynx|

---
## Diagnostic performance
- Sensitivity ~**85-90%** for single adenoma localisation
- **Superior to US and sestamibi** for ectopic and re-operative cases
- **Reduced sensitivity for multiglandular hyperplasia** (smaller, less avid glands)

---
## Pitfalls
- Intrathyroidal adenoma - easily missed
- Multigland disease - dominant gland may mask smaller ones
- FOV too limited - miss deep mediastinal ectopic gland
- Thyroid nodule mimicking adenoma (especially hyperenhancing follicular lesions)
- **Thyroid radiation dose** - especially young/pregnant patients; counsel and consider reduced-phase protocol

---
> [!tip] Exam pearls
> 
> - **Classic triad**: low NC + avid arterial + washout on venous/delayed
> - **Adenoma behaves opposite to thyroid on NC** (thyroid hyperdense from iodine)
> - **Polar vessel sign** = prominent feeder, highly suggestive
> - **Re-operative neck**: 4D CT is the modality of choice
> - Most common ectopic site for **inferior** adenoma = **anterior mediastinum / thymus**; for **superior** = retro-oesophageal / tracheo-oesophageal groove
