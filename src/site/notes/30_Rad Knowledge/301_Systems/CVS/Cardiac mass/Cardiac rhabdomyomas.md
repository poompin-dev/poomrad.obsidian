- Foetal/neonatal intramural or intracavitary benign masses; often multiple; tend to **regress** spontaneously.
	- Can cause flow obstruction esp. if near LVOT.
- **Imaging:** 
	- Echocardiography, usually diagnostics and also used for follow up until regress.
	- CMR if needed for tissue characterisation and flow/obstruction.
