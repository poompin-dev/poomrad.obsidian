[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Tuberculous osteomyelitis\|Tuberculous osteomyelitis]] involving the short tubular bones of the hands/feet in children.
## Appearance
Expansile, cystic destruction of a phalanx with soft tissue swelling.
## Differential diagnosis
- Syphilis
- Sickle cell dactylitis (Hand-foot syndrome).
