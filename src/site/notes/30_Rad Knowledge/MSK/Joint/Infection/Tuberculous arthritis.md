## Phemister's triad
Classic description of tuberculous arthritis:
1.  **Severe juxta-articular osteoporosis** (hyperaemia).
2.  **Peripheral bony erosions** (marginal).
3.  **Gradual narrowing of joint space** (cartilage is preserved until late stages, unlike pyogenic where it is destroyed early).
