## Physiological basis
* **Vascularity:** Rich blood supply (terminal loops of nutrient arteries).
    * *Consequence:* Predilection for **haematogenous** spread (metastasis, infection).
* **Growth:** Area of highest metabolic activity and turnover.
    * *Consequence:* Site of origin for most primary bone tumours (osteosarcoma, chondrosarcoma).
---
## Differential diagnosis approach
*Diagnosis relies on **age** + **position** (central vs eccentric) + **matrix**.*
### A. Non-aggressive / benign ("leave me alone" lesions)
| Lesion                              | Position                 | Typical age | Key imaging features                                                                                                                                       |
| :---------------------------------- | :----------------------- | :---------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Simple Bone Cyst\|Simple bone cyst]]** (UBC)      | **Central**              | < 20        | • "Fallen fragment" sign (pathognomonic)<br>• Truncated cone shape<br>• No periosteal reaction unless fractured                                            |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Non-ossifying fibroma\|Non-ossifying fibroma]]** / FCD | **Cortical / eccentric** | < 30        | • "Bubbly" lytic lesion with sclerotic rim<br>• Distal femur/proximal tibia<br>• Migrates away from physis with growth                                     |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Chondromyxoid fibroma\|Chondromyxoid fibroma]]** (CMF) | **Eccentric**            | 10–30       | • Lytic, lobulated, sclerotic rim<br>• "Bite" out of the bone                                                                                              |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Enchondroma\|Enchondroma]]**                 | **Central**              | 20–50       | • Chondroid matrix (rings & arcs)<br>• Scalloping of endosteum < 50% depth                                                                                 |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Brodie's abscess\|Brodie's abscess]]**            | **Central / eccentric**  | < 25        | • Lytic with thick sclerotic rim<br>• **Penumbra sign** (T1 hyperintense rim on MRI) indicating granulation tissue<br>• *Thai context:* Always rule out TB |

### B. Aggressive / malignant / locally invasive
| Lesion                                         | Position                               | Typical age           | Key imaging features                                                                                                               |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Malignant/Osteosarcoma\|Osteosarcoma]]**                           | **Central / eccentric**                | 10–25                 | • **Sunburst** / Codman's triangle<br>• Osteoid matrix (cloud-like)<br>• *Variant:* [[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Malignant/Telangiectatic osteosarcoma\|Telangiectatic osteosarcoma]] (fluid-levels) |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Aneurysmal bone cyst\|Aneurysmal bone cyst]]** (ABC)             | **Eccentric**                          | < 20                  | • **Fluid-fluid levels** (MRI/CT)<br>• Expansile "blown out" cortex<br>• Can be primary or secondary to GCT/osteoblastoma          |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Giant cell tumour\|Giant cell tumour]]** (GCT)                | **Eccentric $\rightarrow$ epiphyseal** | 20–40 (Closed physis) | • **Abuts articular surface** (subchondral)<br>• Non-sclerotic margin<br>• "Soap bubble" appearance                                |
| **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Malignant/Chondrosarcoma\|Chondrosarcoma]]**                         | **Central**                            | > 40                  | • Deep endosteal scalloping (>2/3)<br>• Cortical breakthrough + soft tissue mass<br>• Popcorn calcification                        |
| **Metastasis / [[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Malignant/Multiple myeloma\|myeloma]]** | Any                                    | > 40                  | • Moth-eaten/permeative<br>• No sclerotic rim (unless prostate/breast/treated)                                                     |

## High-yield exam pearls

> [!NOTE] The "fluid-level" trap
> Fluid-fluid levels are **not** specific for ABC.
> **DDx:**
> 1.  [[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Aneurysmal bone cyst\|ABC]] (primary)
> 2.  **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Malignant/Telangiectatic osteosarcoma\|Telangiectatic osteosarcoma]]** (must biopsy viable tissue, not fluid)
> 3.  [[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Giant cell tumour\|GCT]] (secondary ABC formation)

> [!TIP] "Do not touch" lesions
> If a metaphyseal lesion is:
> * Eccentric
> * Cortically based
> * Sclerotic rimmed
> * Asymptomatic
>
> It is almost certainly a **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Non-ossifying fibroma\|NOF]]**. Do not biopsy.

> [!WARNING] Infection mimicry
> In the Thai Board exam, a solitary metaphyseal lytic lesion with a sclerotic rim in a child is **[[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Brodie's abscess\|Brodie's abscess]]** until proven otherwise.
> * Look for a **serpiginous tract** extending toward the physis.
