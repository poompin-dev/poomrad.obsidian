## Triad
1.  **Multiple NOFs** (often extra-gnathic).
2.  **Café-au-lait spots** (Coast of California).
3.  **Intellectual disability** or hypogonadism.

**Exam tip:** If you see bilateral or multiple [[30_Rad Knowledge/MSK/Bone/Bony lesions/Lytic lesions/Benign/Non-ossifying fibroma\|NOF]]s in a case, look at the skin or clinical notes for [[30_Rad Knowledge/Multisystem/Neoplasms/Tumor predisposition syndromes/Neurofibromatosis type 1\|NF1]]-like features (but this is distinct from NF1).
