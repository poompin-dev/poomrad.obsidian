## Romanus Lesion
- **Definition:** Corner inflammatory lesion (vertebral endplate)
- **Imaging:** Early: T2/STIR high signal (MRI), later sclerosis (X-ray/CT, "shiny corner")
- **Significance:** Earliest spinal change in AS, reflects active inflammation
## Shiny Corner Sign
- **Definition:** Sclerosis at vertebral corners (healed Romanus lesion)
- **Imaging:** Sclerotic vertebral endplate corners on X-ray
## Syndesmophyte
- **Definition:** Thin, vertical, marginal new bone bridging vertebrae
- **Imaging:** "Bamboo spine" in late AS
## Andersson Lesion
- **Definition:** Spondylodiscitis-like discovertebral erosion in chronic AS
- **Imaging:** Endplate destruction, disc space irregularity, signal change (MRI/X-ray)
## Other
- **Vertebral body squaring:** Loss of normal anterior concavity, seen in early AS
- **Dagger sign:** Ossification of interspinous/supraspinous ligament (late AS)
