## Key changes from [[30_MSK/Bones/Insight/Lodwick classification\|original]]:
- IC → 2 (geographic, ill-defined)
- II, III → 3B (moth-eaten/permeative)
- Add 3A, 3C (new concepts)
	- **3A (change over time)** = red flag for malignancy: always compare with old images + look for margin change
	- **3C (occult):** remember that radiograph-negative does NOT mean benign
