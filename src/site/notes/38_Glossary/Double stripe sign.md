![[Double stripe sign.jpg\|400]]
**Diffuse, symmetrical increased uptake** along metadiaphysial cortex of long bones, usually bilateral tibias.

Seen in both [[30_MSK/Bones/Bony lesions/Sclerotic lesions/Primary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy\|Pachydermoperiostosis]] and [[30_MSK/Bones/Bony lesions/Sclerotic lesions/Secondary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy\|Secondary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy]].

also called "tramline sign" or "parallel track sign"
