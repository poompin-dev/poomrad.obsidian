## Radiology perspective
In practice, **myasthenia gravis is mainly an “imaging the thymus” disease**.

---
## Associations
### Common
- **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Chest/Mediastinum/Thymoma\|Thymoma]]** (anterior mediastinal mass; classic association) 
- **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Chest/Mediastinum/Thymic rebound\|Thymic hyperplasia]]**
### Uncommon
- **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Chest/Mediastinum/Thymolipoma\|Thymolipoma]]**: fat-containing thymic tumour

---

> [!warning] Pitfalls
> - **Small cell lung cancer** is classically linked to **Lambert-Eaton**, not MG (different NMJ problem, easy to mix up).
> - **[[30_Rad Knowledge/301_Systems/Chest/Mediastinum/Thymic carcinoma\|Thymic carcinoma]]** (MG association is **very rare**, largely case reports; don’t treat it as a typical pairing)
